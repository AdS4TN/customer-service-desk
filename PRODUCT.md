# AgentDesk

## Register

product

## Users

Customer service and sales teams receiving inquiries across website and messaging channels.

## Product Purpose

Bring customer conversations into one inbox with AI assistance and human takeover.

## Brand Personality

Professional, restrained, practical. Preserve the existing AgentDesk dashboard.

## Anti-references

No demo dashboards, decorative metrics, marketing layouts, or unrelated navigation redesigns.

## Design Principles

- Reuse existing channel configuration and reception workflows.
- Show actual connection and delivery state, never simulated success.
- Preserve drafts and provide actionable recovery after failures.
- Keep historical imports separate from live AI dispatch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Chinese and English, keyboard navigation, visible focus, accessible labels, narrow viewports, and reduced motion as required by AGENTS.md.
